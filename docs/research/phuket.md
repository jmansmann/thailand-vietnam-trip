# Phuket research

Research snapshot: 2026-10-20. Prices are planning estimates in **USD per person** unless marked otherwise. Dec 29–Jan 1 is peak holiday demand; rates and minimum stays are date-dependent.

## Where to stay

| Area | Fit for this group | Trade-offs and approximate travel |
|---|---|---|
| **Kamala — recommendation** | Beach village with restaurants, massages, a quieter pace, and access to Catch Beach Club and Surin. Best balance for jet lag, one boat day and NYE. | Roughly 30–45 min from HKT by car; Patong/Bangla Road is a short but hilly drive, typically 20–30 min depending on traffic. |
| **Surin** | Polished, upscale, close to Kamala and Catch; good if beach access is the priority. | Small village, fewer everyday options and limited nightlife beyond beach venues. HKT transfer roughly 30–45 min. |
| **Bang Tao / Laguna** | Long beach, resort services, several restaurants and larger villas; Catch is at the south end of the bay. | Sprawling area; a car or resort shuttle is often needed. HKT transfer roughly 25–40 min; Patong is farther than from Kamala. |
| **Patong** | Best walkable nightlife, dining and broad hotel choice; Bangla Road is on foot. | Busy and commercial, less restorative after the long-haul arrival. Around 35–45 min from HKT. |
| **Kata** | Attractive beach, relaxed restaurants and some nightlife. | South of the other options, so boat-marina and Bang Tao-club transfers take longer. Around 45–60 min from HKT. |

### Lodging for five

- **Villa (recommended if beds are confirmed):** Request five separate beds in writing, room-by-room bed configuration, refundable deposit, staff/chef options, late check-in, and NYE minimum-stay/cancellation terms. Planning range for a high-end 4–5-bedroom pool villa over NYE: **~$1,200–4,000/night total** (**~$240–800 pp/night**). Hold **5–7 nights** as a possible minimum until confirmed; a 3-bedroom villa may require sharing.
- **Hotel:** Book three rooms (two twins and a single, depending on inventory) or a multi-bedroom suite with individual beds confirmed. More service and simpler logistics, less shared space.
- **Named high-end options:** **Andara Resort & Villas** (Kamala; suites and 3–7-bedroom private-pool villas), **Rosewood Phuket** (Emerald Bay, south of Patong; resort villas/houses), and **Twinpalms Surin Beach Phuket** (Surin). Treat availability, final rates and bed layout as **verify**. Kamala is a more practical base than Emerald Bay for Catch / Patong rides.

## New Year's Eve beach clubs

- **Catch Beach Club, Bang Tao:** Its official site currently shows a **Festive Season 26/27** section. It is the strongest current shortlist option for a Kamala/Bang Tao stay. The ordinary daybed/sala minimum spend shown on the site is roughly ฿6,000–8,000 for 1–3 guests with food-and-beverage credit; these are **not NYE prices**.
- **Café del Mar Phuket, Kamala:** Plausible fit because it is in the preferred base area, but its site did not load successfully during research. Current operation and event details are **verify**.
- **Xana / Bimi / Kudo / Ilha Verde / other Patong options:** Keep as alternatives only if current 2026/27 programming, location, and ticket terms are confirmed. The Patong guide supports Bangla Road as the main nightlife district, not each venue's current operation.
- **Planning allowance (not a published 2026 price):** Hold **~$150–400 pp** for entry / dinner package or **~$250–1,000+ pp** for shared table / premium package. Table minimums are per table and may be four figures or more. Confirm tax, service, food/drink credit, seating, arrival window and refund terms. Book once the official page opens.
- **Backup:** Reserve dinner near the villa, then use a pre-booked driver for a local countdown / public beach visit. Do not assume rides will be available after midnight.

### Phuket nights

Keep **all three Phuket nights (Dec 29, 30, and 31) at the same property**. Do not schedule an overnight excursion away from the Phuket base. Phi Phi, Railay/Krabi, and Koh Yao Noi remain possible day-trip destinations only if the group picks an appropriate boat option; a separate overnight would cost too much recovery time on this short stop.

## Food and nightlife

- Southern Thai dishes to try: **massaman curry, khanom jeen with curry, gaeng tai pla, moo hong (Phuket-style braised pork), fresh seafood, roti with curry**, and local Hokkien noodles.
- Old Town shortlist: **Raya** (southern Thai; crab curry / mee hoon), **Blue Elephant Phuket** (upscale Thai in a historic mansion), and **Kra Jok See** (book ahead; lively late dinner). Confirm holiday openings and reservations.
- Kamala is mostly relaxed beach bars and restaurants. **Bangla Road, Patong** is the main late-night strip; make it one optional night, not the sleep base. Catch is the closest beach-club option to the planned base. Old Town offers cocktail bars and a more local evening.

## Getting around

- **HKT → Kamala:** Roughly 30–45 min by car, longer with holiday traffic. For five and luggage, pre-book a van or two cars; confirm luggage capacity and fixed fare.
- **Grab / Bolt:** Useful for point-to-point rides, but driver supply and pickup points vary by beach and late at night. Do not rely on one app ride after NYE.
- **Private driver:** Best for a sightseeing day or group dinner across the island. Agree on hours, overtime, tolls and pickup time.
- **Phuket Smart Bus / local buses:** Low cost along the west coast, but slower, limited at night, and not ideal when all five are moving together.
- **Motorbike rental:** Not recommended for this group. Valid motorcycle licence/IDP and insurance matter; crash risk and rental disputes outweigh the convenience.

## Verify before booking

- Villa/hotel availability, five separate beds, minimum stay, late check-in, cancellation, and final total including taxes.
- Catch / Café del Mar / other club NYE event format, official tickets, table minimums, age/bag policy and return transport. Catch's fetched site showed the festive-season section but not NYE details.
- Similan, Maya Bay and Phang Nga Bay dates/fees; sea conditions, Maya Bay access and swimming rules.
- Elephant venue welfare policy; whether the visit involves riding or bathing.
- Big Buddha, Wat Chalong, restaurants, cooking classes and muay-thai hours / holiday availability.
- Boat tour inclusions, unsafe-weather policy, driver quote and vehicle capacity.
