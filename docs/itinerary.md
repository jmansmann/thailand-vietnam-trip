# Thailand + Vietnam itinerary draft

**For review — 2026-10-20.** Five travelers; flights excluded from the budget. Every price below is a rough **USD per-person estimate** unless labeled per room / group. Prices are not quotes; NYE, holiday hours, visa rules, and all other time-sensitive details are **(verify)** before booking.

## Summary

| Stop | Nights | Dates | Base | Stay recommendation |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Phuket | 3 | Dec 29–Jan 1 | Kamala | One property for all three nights, with five separate beds confirmed. Group decision stays open: pool villa vs. hotel, with Andara Resort & Villas as a named hotel option. |
| Bangkok | 3 | Jan 1–4 | Sukhumvit / Asok | Three rooms or suite + connecting twin room; choose rail access and quiet rooms. |
| Hanoi | 5 total (4 hotel + 1 cruise) | Jan 4–9 | Old Quarter / Hoan Kiem | Three rooms or connecting rooms; retain the Hanoi room during cruise night if the rate is reasonable. One night aboard a Lan Ha Bay cruise. |

**Route:** Phuket → Bangkok → Hanoi → Lan Ha Bay → Hanoi. All three Phuket nights are at the same Kamala property; there is no Phuket overnight excursion. No other city/base changes are recommended beyond the scheduled cruise night. The Hanoi order stays as decided: city day, Ninh Binh day trip, then Lan Ha cruise.

## Day by day

### Tue Dec 29 — Phuket landing: pool, massage, and an early dinner

- **Morning:** Land HKT around 08:00; pre-book a van for five with luggage to Kamala. Ask the villa to confirm early bag drop / early check-in; do not assume the rooms will be ready.
- **Afternoon:** Pool, an easy beach walk, optional Thai massage, groceries and water for the villa. Keep plans flexible after the overnight journey.
- **Evening:** Dinner at **PP&P** on Kamala Beach (or a villa chef if everyone is exhausted); book / confirm hours for five.
- **Chill alternative:** Stay at the pool, order dinner in, and call it an early night.
- **Estimate:** **$350–950 pp** including first night lodging, transfer, meals, and optional massage.
- **Booking notes:** Airport-to-villa van and villa check-in / baggage plan; verify separate beds and pool safety / access.

### Wed Dec 30 — Phang Nga Bay by boat

- **Morning:** Breakfast at the villa, then early pickup for a Phang Nga Bay small-group or private boat day with sea-cave canoeing. Choose a program that includes a licensed canoe guide and clarify national-park fees.
- **Afternoon:** Kayak / canoe through permitted sea caves and lagoons; make a short James Bond Island stop if the group wants it, while expecting crowds. Return to Kamala by late afternoon.
- **Evening:** Dinner at the villa or back at **PP&P** in Kamala; no late night before NYE. Lunch is normally included in the boat package—confirm before paying.
- **Chill alternative:** Skip the boat and spend the day between Kamala Beach, massage, and the villa pool.
- **Estimate:** **$400–1,050 pp** including lodging, boat tour, meals, and local transport.
- **Booking notes:** Compare a small-group canoe tour against a private speedboat for five. Confirm route, group size, departure time, fees, weather cancellation, and return time.

### Thu Dec 31 — Kamala reset, then NYE beach club

- **Morning:** Sleep in, late breakfast, and swim / beach time. Keep the morning light.
- **Afternoon:** Lunch at the villa, then downtime; arrange a confirmed driver and pickup point for the evening.
- **Evening:** NYE at **Catch Beach Club** is the current first choice; compare with Café del Mar only after its 2026/27 event, tickets, and current operation are confirmed. Reserve the club's dinner package or eat at **PP&P** before heading out.
- **Chill alternative / backup:** Private chef or villa dinner, then a nearby bar or public countdown only if transport and crowd plans are clear.
- **Estimate:** **$500–1,800 pp** including lodging, food, event/table spend, and return transport. The event range is a planning allowance, not an announced NYE price.
- **Booking notes:** Secure refundable lodging first, then official NYE tickets / table and transport. Keep ticket, ID, pickup location, and post-midnight plan offline.

### Fri Jan 1 — Late morning recovery; afternoon Phuket → Bangkok

- **Morning:** Late breakfast at the villa, hydrate, pack, and check out. Do not schedule a beach excursion after NYE.
- **Afternoon:** Domestic transfer to Bangkok (flight details are out of scope); pre-book a car/van from the airport to the Asok hotel.
- **Evening:** Easy dinner at **Thipsamai** if the group has energy and arrival timing works; otherwise eat at the hotel. Optional rooftop drink only with a confirmed booking.
- **Chill alternative:** Room service, pool, or an early night.
- **Estimate:** **$250–700 pp** including lodging, meals, and ground transport; domestic airfare excluded.
- **Booking notes:** Confirm hotel check-in, bed setup, and which Bangkok airport the inbound flight uses. Leave a generous buffer because BKK and DMK are separate airports.

### Sat Jan 2 — Chatuchak, then Chinatown dinner

- **Morning:** Breakfast at the hotel, then go to Chatuchak Weekend Market near opening while it is cooler. Set a meeting point and time in case anyone splits off.
- **Afternoon:** Lunch from Chatuchak's market stalls; return to the hotel for a break, then choose a nearby museum, massage, or pool.
- **Evening:** Explore Yaowarat street food and sit down at **T&K Seafood** if the queue is reasonable; keep the group together crossing traffic.
- **Chill alternative:** Skip the market, have brunch, visit Jim Thompson House, and keep Chinatown for dinner.
- **Estimate:** **$200–500 pp** including lodging, local transport, food, admissions, and modest shopping.
- **Booking notes:** Chatuchak normally operates on weekends; verify the Jan 2 schedule and current hours. No pre-booked tour required.

### Sun Jan 3 — Grand Palace, Wat Pho, Wat Arun; rooftop or Muay Thai

- **Morning:** Breakfast at the hotel, then early Grand Palace / Wat Phra Kaew entry with shoulders and legs covered. Allow for security and heat.
- **Afternoon:** Wat Pho, lunch at a riverside café near Tha Tien, then cross the river to Wat Arun by ferry. Return to Asok for rest.
- **Evening:** Group choice: rooftop cocktails at **Vertigo & Moon Bar** (or Octave / Sky Bar / Tichuca; reservation and dress code **verify**) or a Muay Thai bout (event schedule **verify**). Dinner at **Le Du** if the group chooses a tasting menu and secures a reservation; otherwise book a shared Thai dinner near Asok.
- **Chill alternative:** Do only the Grand Palace + Wat Pho, then take a riverboat and have dinner near the hotel.
- **Estimate:** **$250–800 pp** including lodging, local transport, temple admissions, meals, and optional evening activity.
- **Booking notes:** Check official hours and closures at the entrance; reject “the palace is closed” touts unless confirmed by the palace itself.

### Mon Jan 4 — Bangkok → Hanoi; arrival afternoon

- **Morning:** Breakfast at the Bangkok hotel, check out, and transfer to the airport for the scheduled flight (flight research excluded). Carry travel documents and Vietnam entry approval offline.
- **Afternoon:** Arrive HAN, use a pre-arranged hotel transfer or app car to the Old Quarter / Hoan Kiem. Check in and rest.
- **Evening:** Short Hoan Kiem Lake loop, egg coffee at **Cafe Giang**, and an early **Bun Cha Huong Lien** dinner if arrival timing works.
- **Chill alternative:** Stay in, order a simple meal, and adjust sleep after travel.
- **Estimate:** **$220–650 pp** including Hanoi lodging, meals, airport transfer and local transport; airfare excluded.
- **Booking notes:** Confirm Hanoi hotel late arrival and the correct Hanoi airport pickup point. Vietnam e-visa requirements are **verify**; see practicals.

### Tue Jan 5 — Hanoi history and a guided food evening

- **Morning:** Breakfast at **Pho Gia Truyen Bat Dan**, then Hoan Kiem Lake / Ngoc Son Temple and the Temple of Literature. If the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum is open and the group wants it, swap it in after checking schedule and dress/security rules.
- **Afternoon:** Lunch at **Cha Ca Thang Long**, then Hoa Lo Prison or the Vietnam Museum of Ethnology (the latter requires a car and more time). Build in a rest stop.
- **Evening:** Small-group motorbike street-food tour with professional drivers, or a market-visit cooking class. Finish with egg coffee at **Cafe Giang** if it fits the tour. Choose one activity rather than stacking both.
- **Chill alternative:** Old Quarter wander, coffee tasting, and a water-puppet show.
- **Estimate:** **$180–500 pp** including lodging, admissions, meals and one guided food activity.
- **Booking notes:** Confirm attraction hours, any Monday/Friday Mausoleum closure, tour pickup, helmet / driver inclusion, and cancellation policy.

### Wed Jan 6 — Ninh Binh: Tràng An boat and Hang Múa

- **Morning:** Breakfast from the hotel or a takeaway, then private van leaves Hanoi early (around 06:30–07:00). Take the Tràng An boat route; expect a multi-hour rowboat loop through caves and temple stops.
- **Afternoon:** Lunch at **Thang Long Restaurant** near Tràng An, then climb Hang Múa if weather and energy allow. Private van back to Hanoi after an early meal.
- **Evening:** Return to the hotel and pack a small cruise bag; leave larger luggage at the hotel if the cruise operator confirms secure storage.
- **Chill alternative:** Boat trip only, with a slower lunch and skip the 500-step Hang Múa climb.
- **Estimate:** **$250–600 pp** including lodging, private vehicle/guide, admissions, meals and transport.
- **Booking notes:** Confirm route and boat admission, Hang Múa ticket, driver overtime, restroom stops, and forecast. This is a long day; wear grippy walking shoes.

### Thu Jan 7 — Hanoi to Lan Ha Bay; overnight aboard

- **Morning:** Breakfast at the hotel, then cruise shuttle pickup (typically around 08:00); transfer toward Tuan Chau / cruise embarkation point. Bring a warm layer, sunscreen, and dry bag.
- **Afternoon:** Board around noon, lunch on board, cruise through Lan Ha Bay, and kayak / visit a village or cave according to the final itinerary.
- **Evening:** Sunset on deck, dinner in the ship's dining room and overnight aboard. The planned boat should have private cabins; check room configuration for all five travelers.
- **Chill alternative:** Stay in Hanoi and use this as a free day if the group chooses to skip the overnight cruise.
- **Estimate:** **$400–950 pp** including cruise, meals, transfer, and Hanoi hotel night if retained. Do not double-count the lodging line if cruise package pricing already includes it.
- **Booking notes:** Cruise is a top-priority booking. Compare Heritage Line Ginger and Orchid Premium / Classic. Confirm the actual vessel, pickup/drop-off, cabin plan, inclusions, port fees, weather policy, and timely return.

### Fri Jan 8 — Morning kayak, return to Hanoi, weekend walking street

- **Morning:** Optional early tai chi / kayak and breakfast in the ship's dining room; disembark about 11:00–11:30.
- **Afternoon:** Return to Hanoi, expected around 14:00–15:00; check into the retained room or collect stored luggage. Rest and shower.
- **Evening:** Hoan Kiem walking street begins Friday night under its usual schedule; **verify** the current holiday calendar. Farewell dinner at **Gia** or **Tầm Vị** if a table is secured; then a short Ta Hien / cocktail-bar stop for those who want it.
- **Chill alternative:** Skip nightlife and have dinner near the hotel; keep the room early for the flight home.
- **Estimate:** **$200–600 pp** including lodging, cruise transfer, dinner, local transport and optional drinks.
- **Booking notes:** Confirm arrival time in Hanoi and hotel luggage arrangements. Do not plan a fixed-time activity between disembarkation and the evening.

### Sat Jan 9 — Hanoi departure

- **Morning:** Breakfast at the hotel or the Metropole's **Le Club Bar** for a sit-down option, check out, and pre-book a van / two cars to HAN. Add a generous road and airport buffer.
- **Afternoon / evening:** Scheduled return to NYC (flight research excluded).
- **Chill alternative:** Not applicable; keep the airport transfer conservative.
- **Estimate:** **$80–250 pp** for breakfast, hotel if a late checkout / extra night is selected, and ground transport; airfare excluded.
- **Booking notes:** Confirm airline terminal, pickup time, passport, e-visa / entry record copies, and all luggage before leaving.
